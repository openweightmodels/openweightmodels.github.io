Method

Guides inherit the registry's evidence rules.

A guide can organize facts around a use case without turning editorial convenience into a new model fact.

Hardware categories are navigation aids, not guaranteed minimum requirements. Commercial-use labels summarize the recorded license state but do not replace the official license. Provider availability is treated as a deployment record and can change independently of model weights.

Every guide therefore links to the underlying passport and the Methodology page, making the path from summary to primary evidence visible.
