The key distinction

Commercial use is a license field, not an “open” synonym.

Two models can both publish downloadable weights and still have materially different commercial conditions.

Apache 2.0 and MIT are standard permissive licenses represented heavily in this registry. Other releases use custom terms. Llama Community licenses include model-specific redistribution and scale clauses; Gemma uses its own Terms of Use; Qwen2.5-VL uses a Qwen license; Kimi K2 uses a modified MIT license with an additional scale-triggered branding provision.

OpenWeightModels therefore stores commercial use separately from weight availability, modification rights, redistribution, incorporated use policies and deployment-provider terms. A green “commercial use” field should never be treated as the complete legal analysis.

FAQ

Commercial-use questions.

Which open-weight models allow commercial use?

In the current 32-model registry, 23 models are recorded as commercially allowed under Apache 2.0 or MIT, one is allowed with a special condition, and eight use conditional custom terms. The exact license record should be checked before deployment.

Does open weight mean commercial use is allowed?

No. Weight availability and commercial permission are separate attributes. Custom licenses can add attribution, scale, redistribution, geography or acceptable-use conditions.

Are Apache 2.0 and MIT the simplest commercial licenses in the registry?

They are the main standard permissive licenses represented here. They generally permit commercial use, modification and redistribution subject to their license conditions.

Is this commercial-use page legal advice?

No. It is a technical comparison of recorded license terms. The official license, incorporated policies, provider terms and applicable law remain authoritative.
